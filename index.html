Minecraft; More than just a game

Many people, namely parents, educators and maturing children, believe that Minecraft is simply just a game. It may be distracting, difficult or even boring to many people who never played the game, and it doesnt look very apealing to many new players.

Well, dear reader, you came to the right place. In this article, I will be covering the many benefits of minecraft, the gameplay as well as why you should play or at least try to play this game. First of all, The game teaches you to become more social, more collabrative. Not just with your friends and family, with every individual around you. Why is this the case? Well, you see, Minecraft allows you to play not just alone, but with other people. Wether they're right next to you or on the other side of the globe, you could always interact and play with them in personal or community servers. You could explore, fight, build and have fun with your friends or complete strangers! This function could help you make more friends. Another one of the many benefits of Minecraft is the creativity and freedom it allows. According to the research institute of Oxford, many of the current architects use Minecraft to create pre-prototypes to test out they're projects!
